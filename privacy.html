Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you visit this website or use the Zorvia apps: Zorvia IPTV Pro (Android, Android TV, Fire TV and Windows, downloaded from this website) and IPTV Zorvia Player (the free edition on Google Play). In short: we collect as little as possible, we never sell data, and your playlists stay on your device.

1. Your playlists and logins

The M3U links and Xtream Codes logins you add are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. The app connects directly from your device to the provider you entered.

2. Licence check (Zorvia IPTV Pro only)

To manage the free trial and the lifetime activation, the app sends this to our licence server when it starts:

  • a device code such as ZRV-XXXX-XXXX. It is calculated from your device identifier with a one-way function, so the identifier itself is not sent;
  • the app version and edition;
  • the country of the connection, worked out by our hosting provider from the network address. The network address itself is not stored;
  • the date of the first and last check, the licence status, and the activation code if you have one.

We use this only to run the trial and the activation, to stop abuse of activation codes, and to count how many devices use the app. An activated device must reach the server at least once every 3 days.

3. This website

  • When you open the site or press a download button, we count the visit or the download with its country, the page and the date. We do not store your network address and we use no cookies. Your browser stores one small value (today's date) so that you are counted once a day.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our counter and licence server run on Cloudflare. These providers process network addresses to deliver and protect their services.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.

4. Buying an activation

When you contact us on WhatsApp to buy an activation, we see your phone number and the messages you send, and we may note your name and the payment date next to your device code. Payments by bank transfer or Wise are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.

5. Free edition on Google Play (ads)

The free edition shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings. Zorvia IPTV Pro (the paid edition) has no ads.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it except with the service providers named above, or when the law requires it. Licence records are kept while the licence exists. Visit counters keep only the country and date.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data linked to your device code at any time. Contact us on WhatsApp with your device code (shown in the app under Activate). If we delete an activated device, its activation is cancelled.

8. Children

The apps and this site are not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
