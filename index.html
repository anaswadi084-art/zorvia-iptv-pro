FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Zorvia IPTV Pro include channels or films?

No. It is a media player only and comes with no content. You add playlists from a provider you are legally allowed to use.

Which formats are supported?

M3U / M3U8 links (including get.php links), Xtream Codes logins, HLS and MPEG-TS streams, MP4 and MKV files.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

The app asks for an activation code. Activation costs 5€ once and works for life on that device.

I changed my phone or TV. Do I pay again?

Each activation is linked to one device. Contact us on WhatsApp with your new device code.

How do I update the app?

The app tells you when a new version is ready. Install it over the old one; your playlists and activation stay.
