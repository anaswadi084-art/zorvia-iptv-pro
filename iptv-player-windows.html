FAQ

Is Zorvia available for Mac or iPhone?

Not yet. Zorvia works on Windows, Android, Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV.

Can I use the same playlist on my PC and my TV?

Yes. Add the same M3U link or Xtream Codes login on each device. Each device has its own licence.

Does Zorvia include channels?

No. Zorvia is a media player only. You add your own playlist.
